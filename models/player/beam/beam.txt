Scientists boosted every attribute way beyond anything the world has ever seen.  These 15 prototypes are the ulitimate in warfare, they combine lightning fast speed with incredible reflexes, giving them deadly accuracy.  They see using ultra infared and nightvision. Their armour is made of an alien alloy discovered on one of the moons of jupiter, and is inpenitrable.  Their strength is unrivaled and have on numerous occasions killed armies of more then 500 in 2 minutes!  Without a scratch.  If there is ever one of thes bio-assasins after you, you are already dead!
